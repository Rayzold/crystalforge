This is what the roads say. Ask in person for the rest.

Heroes

Figures of standing. A party can expect help, on their terms.

Tolmara Bell-Veined

The Ashenborn Who Holds the Gate · she/her

Crackglass · Guardian Initiate · Guardian Fighter 11

What is known

An Ashenborn in Guardian colours, which stops conversations. She holds the eastern gate at Memento and has done for six years without an incident anyone can pin on her. Her veins whistle when she walks, so you hear her before you see her, and she has stopped apologising for it.

What they can do for you

She will vouch for you at any Guardian outpost, and her word carries further than her rank does. She hands out her Skyquake Earplugs to anyone heading into the Outer Lands — she has no use for them and considers it rude to say why.

Ilve Eighty-Eight

Cataloguer of the Numbered List · she/her

Wonderborn · Wonder Cataloguer · Gated Magic Wizard 14

What is known

She has catalogued thirty-one of the thirty-nine found Wonders in person and will tell you about every one of them at length. She is the reason four of them are numbered correctly. Nobody in the Observatory likes her and all of them cite her.

What they can do for you

She can put a party inside a sealed Old World installation and get them out again, and she will do it for access rather than coin. If you have stood somewhere she has not, she wants to hear about it.

Grath Three-Chieftains

Who Outlived the Tribe · he/him

Slowburn · Ashenborn Outcast · Oath of the Scar Paladin 12

What is known

An Ashenborn paladin, which the cities find funnier than he does. He has buried three Chieftains he was younger than, and after the third he stopped going back. He now serves an oath rather than a tribe, and he will explain the difference if you have an hour.

What they can do for you

He is the only person most parties will meet who can walk into an Ashenborn war camp and walk out with terms. He asks for nothing and accepts food, which is the Ashenborn form and he keeps it.

Nix Foundwell

Weatherwright of the Iron Caravan · they/them

Salvagekin · Observatory Watcher · Observatory Instrumentalist Artificer 9

What is known

The Iron Caravan does not roll on a day Nix says not to, and the Iron Caravan is not sentimental. They read the sky for a living and are correct often enough that three cities buy the bulletin.

What they can do for you

A forecast, to the round, for any route inside twenty-four hours — and they will infuse gear for a party heading somewhere they have advised against, while continuing to advise against it.

The Useful and the Unaligned

Services, contacts, and people who are nobody’s ally.

Kaelmar Bright-Jaw

The Face Worth Money · he/him

Diamondknit · Ashenborn Outcast · Reclaimer Rogue 7

What is known

The Ashenborn who does business in Memento, with a seam of set glass across his jaw that people cannot stop looking at. He moves goods between the volcanic country and the cities and is scrupulous about the forms, which is why he is still alive.

What they can do for you

He can buy or sell anything that came out of the Otherside, arrange a parley with a war band, and tell you within a sentence whether the person across the table intends to pay.

Aldous Vane

Called the Doctor · he/him

The Kept · Chrono Fog Returnee · Spark Domain Cleric 13

What is known

The Doctor refused the Guardians, in writing, and the letter has been copied so many times that there are forgeries. He treats anyone, including people the cities have outlawed, and he has never once been caught with a patient he could not justify.

What they can do for you

Stabilisation at range, a whole room triaged in minutes, and a lost limb or sense restored — four times, and then the Shard is spent. He asks no questions about how you came by the injury.

Torgash Door-Opener

The Reclaimers’ Key · he/him

Wroughtblood · Brimstone Warden · Line Mechanist Artificer 6

What is known

If a door has not opened in four hundred years, the Reclaimers send Torgash. He puts a hand on it and it remembers how. He charges well and is worth it, and he will not take a job inside three miles of open water without explaining why.

What they can do for you

Entry into anything Old World, a structural map two hundred feet down, and an honest assessment of whether the installation is still powered before anybody goes in.

Selane Dawn

Also Registered as Ashar Dusk · she/her

Dusklit Elf · Memento Duskclerk · College of Two Councils Bard 12

What is known

She is a Memento duskclerk, and she is also a dawnclerk, and both registrations are genuine. She produces documents. Whether a document is legitimate is a question she considers poorly framed.

What they can do for you

A blank genuine writ, a permit that survives inspection, and an introduction to whichever council is currently the useful one. She prices in favours and keeps meticulous count.

Villains

Everyone here has a plan and is not in a hurry.

Ashkora Grey-Under

Who the Shamans Will Not Petition For · she/her

Shroudknit · Wyldermoore Deathwarden · The Scarring Warlock 15

What is known

An Ashenborn who came out of a Necrotic Shroud and did not come out the same. The shamans will not petition on her behalf because they are not certain who would answer. Wyldermoore has a standing warrant and has not served it, because nobody has volunteered to.

What they can do for you

None. She does not sell anything. If she approaches a party it is because she wants something specific from one of them, and she will be pleasant and precise about it.

Corran

Who Answers to Corr, and to Neither · he/him

Fractured · Datasphere Diver · Nanite Bloodline Sorcerer 13

What is known

A Fractured Echoed who goes by Corran and by Corr and corrects nobody. He was a respectable Datasphere diver until about two years ago. People who worked with him then describe the change as gradual and then not.

What they can do for you

He recruits. He is generous, genuinely charming, and very good at identifying the one thing a person has not been offered yet.

Marrek Oathwall

Who Kept the Doctrine · he/him

Bulwark Orc · Guardian Initiate · Echoing Blade Fighter 15

What is known

A Guardian officer of long service and visible decoration, currently holding a settlement in the Outer Lands that the Guardians say is under his protection. The settlement has not sent anyone out in five months. Officially there is no problem.

What they can do for you

He will hear a delegation. He is courteous, he is not lying, and he will explain his reasoning in full, which is the most disturbing part of meeting him.

Ilvess

Not Detectable, Not Remembered · she/her

The Vessin · Memory Broker · Ghost Rogue 11

What is known

There is no public information. Three cities have an open file with no name on it and a consistent method. Two of the files are closed as accidental. The Memory Brokers of Memento will confirm that someone with her description does not work for them, and will not elaborate.

What they can do for you

She takes contracts. There is no way to find her; she finds the client, once, and the conversation happens somewhere the client did not choose.
