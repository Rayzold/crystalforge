The Scarred Lands

The Drift Register

Thirty people you will hear about before you meet. Between them they account for every species and every subclass in the catalogue — and every background, feat, spell and item on these pages exists there too. Follow any link to the full entry.

This is what the roads say. Ask in person for the rest.

Heroes

Figures of standing. A party can expect help, on their terms.

full length

Tolmara Bell-Veined ♀

The Ashenborn Who Holds the Gate

Ashenborn - Crackglass · Guardian Initiate · Guardian Fighter 11

What is known

An Ashenborn in Guardian colours, which stops conversations. She holds the eastern gate at Memento and has done for six years without an incident anyone can pin on her. Her veins whistle when she walks, so you hear her before you see her, and she has stopped apologising for it.

What they can do for you

She will vouch for you at any Guardian outpost, and her word carries further than her rank does. She hands out her Skyquake Earplugs to anyone heading into the Outer Lands — she has no use for them and considers it rude to say why.

Ilve Eighty-Eight ♀

Cataloguer of the Numbered List

Tiefling - Wonderborn · Wonder Cataloguer · Gated Magic Wizard 14

What is known

She has catalogued thirty-one of the thirty-nine found Wonders in person and will tell you about every one of them at length. She is the reason four of them are numbered correctly. Nobody in the Observatory likes her and all of them cite her.

What they can do for you

She can put a party inside a sealed Old World installation and get them out again, and she will do it for access rather than coin. If you have stood somewhere she has not, she wants to hear about it.

Grath Three-Chieftains ♂

Who Outlived the Tribe

Ashenborn - Slowburn · Ashenborn Outcast · Oath of the Scar Paladin 12

What is known

An Ashenborn paladin, which the cities find funnier than he does. He has buried three Chieftains he was younger than, and after the third he stopped going back. He now serves an oath rather than a tribe, and he will explain the difference if you have an hour.

What they can do for you

He is the only person most parties will meet who can walk into an Ashenborn war camp and walk out with terms. He asks for nothing and accepts food, which is the Ashenborn form and he keeps it.

Nix Foundwell ⚲

Weatherwright of the Iron Caravan

Gnome - Salvagekin · Observatory Watcher · Observatory Instrumentalist Artificer 9

What is known

The Iron Caravan does not roll on a day Nix says not to, and the Iron Caravan is not sentimental. They read the sky for a living and are correct often enough that three cities buy the bulletin.

What they can do for you

A forecast, to the round, for any route inside twenty-four hours — and they will infuse gear for a party heading somewhere they have advised against, while continuing to advise against it.

Eshe the Narrow ♀

Who Came Back Through the Gap

The Echoed - Veilwalker · Echoed Emissary · College of the Echoed Bard 10

What is known

An Echoed of the Veilwalker line who negotiates on behalf of Fur Wehn and calls it 'carrying messages'. She arrives in rooms without having entered them, which she insists is a shortcut and not a threat, and she is usually telling the truth.

What they can do for you

She will carry a message anywhere in the Drift and bring back the answer, and she can tell you within a sentence what an object has been used for. She takes payment in questions answered honestly.

Tessik Nine-Knot ⚲

Who Reads the Pattern Under Things

The Echoed - Threadweaver · Memory Broker · Datasphere Scholar Wizard 13

What is known

A Threadweaver who works the memory trade in Memento and is worth every coin. They can look at a room and tell you the order things happened in. They do not shake hands.

What they can do for you

Object reading, testimony verification, and a written question answered per long rest in a hand that is not theirs. They will not read anything belonging to a child.

Halka Tinevein ♀

Who Takes the Lightning On Purpose

Dwarf - Orewrought · Thundermount Machinist · Path of the Stormtouched Barbarian 10

What is known

A Thundermount machinist who walks toward storms rather than away from them, on the grounds that she can hold more of one than the mountain can. Prospecting crews hire her as a lightning rod with opinions.

What they can do for you

She can find ore through stone, restart dormant Old World machinery, and stand in front of the thing that is about to hit you. She charges by the storm, not by the day.

Arakh of the Sheer ♂

Who Goes Down Where Others Go Around

Goliath - Riftshoulder · Deep Delver · Deep Delver Ranger 11

What is known

A Riftshoulder who works the walls of the Scar and the shafts under Thundermount, and has brought back every crew he has taken down except one. He talks about that one if you ask, which most people find harder than not asking.

What they can do for you

He will take a party into any shaft, ruin or canyon and get them out, and he will read the tier of whatever is down there before anyone commits.

Ashka Panebright ♀

Who Mends the Shield She Was Born Under

Dragonborn - Glassscale · Crystal Harvester · Oath of the Obelisk Paladin 13

What is known

A Glassscale of the Glass Rain fields who took the Obelisk oath and works the four city shields on a rotation nobody asked her to keep. Glassscale names are prettier than Emberscale ones and she has never once brought it up, which everyone notices.

What they can do for you

She repairs barriers — city shields, ward-lines, a barn door — in ten minutes flat, and she will teach the repair to anyone who sits still long enough to learn it.

Corr of the Ninth ♂

Who Was Born Inside a Locked Thing

Tiefling - Wonderborn · Lavender Explorer · Oath of the Numbered List Paladin 11

What is known

A Wonderborn paladin who has made it his stated life's work to open all one hundred and eight. Thirty-nine have been found. He has opened four, has been thrown out of two expeditions for it, and is entirely undiscouraged.

What they can do for you

He opens things. Vaults, reliquaries, sealed doors, a strongbox whose owner died without saying the word. He does not charge and he does not take anything out.

Kaelmar Bright-Jaw ♂

The Face Worth Money

Ashenborn - Diamondknit · Ashenborn Outcast · Reclaimer Rogue 7

What is known

The Ashenborn who does business in Memento, with a seam of set glass across his jaw that people cannot stop looking at. He moves goods between the volcanic country and the cities and is scrupulous about the forms, which is why he is still alive.

What they can do for you

He can buy or sell anything that came out of the Otherside, arrange a parley with a war band, and tell you within a sentence whether the person across the table intends to pay.

Aldous Vane ♂

Called the Doctor

Human - The Kept · Chrono Fog Returnee · Spark Domain Cleric 13

What is known

The Doctor refused the Guardians, in writing, and the letter has been copied so many times that there are forgeries. He treats anyone, including people the cities have outlawed, and he has never once been caught with a patient he could not justify.

What they can do for you

Stabilisation at range, a whole room triaged in minutes, and a lost limb or sense restored — four times, and then the Shard is spent. He asks no questions about how you came by the injury.

Torgash Door-Opener ♂

The Reclaimers’ Key

Ashenborn - Wroughtblood · Brimstone Warden · Line Mechanist Artificer 6

What is known

If a door has not opened in four hundred years, the Reclaimers send Torgash. He puts a hand on it and it remembers how. He charges well and is worth it, and he will not take a job inside three miles of open water without explaining why.

What they can do for you

Entry into anything Old World, a structural map two hundred feet down, and an honest assessment of whether the installation is still powered before anybody goes in.

Selane Dawn ♀

Also Registered as Ashar Dusk

Dusklit Elf · Memento Duskclerk · College of Two Councils Bard 12

What is known

She is a Memento duskclerk, and she is also a dawnclerk, and both registrations are genuine. She produces documents. Whether a document is legitimate is a question she considers poorly framed.

What they can do for you

A blank genuine writ, a permit that survives inspection, and an introduction to whichever council is currently the useful one. She prices in favours and keeps meticulous count.

Ferrin ⚲

Also Ferran, Also Fenn

Human - The Outlined · Void-Touched Scholar · Warrior of the Still Hour Monk 11

What is known

Nobody agrees on the name. Three cities have a record of someone who matches, filed under three spellings, and the descriptions do not overlap on a single detail except that they arrived before they were expected.

What they can do for you

They map temporal distortion for anyone crossing Chrono Fog, and they will walk the route first. They ask for nothing and accept a written account of the crossing.

Ivy Reads-Deep ♀

Who the Lights Answer

Human - The Answered · Bog Diver · Circle of the Bog Druid 9

What is known

One of the Answered, born in the Bog and never inclined to leave it. Bog-edge camps send for her when something in the water has stopped behaving. Her skin lights up with the answer a half-second before she says it, so people watch her arms rather than her face.

What they can do for you

She will guide a party through standing water that would otherwise kill them, judge whether a pool is safe, and ask the lights three questions a day on your behalf.

Nemara Rootsong ♀

Who Catches What the Dying Drop

Heartwood Elf · Wyldermoore Deathwarden · Circle of Wyldermoore Druid 12

What is known

A Wyldermoore deathwarden who grafts living wood onto anything that will take it — walls, hulls, a shattered arm. She attends every burial in her district and stands at the back, and the families have stopped finding it strange.

What they can do for you

She will settle a corpse that will not settle, graft a limb or a hull back together, and tell you what happened in a grove by touching the trees that watched.

Merrit Fastfoot ♀

Who Has Never Been Late

Halfling - Routeborn · Iron Caravan Crew · Iron Caravan Outrider Fighter 9

What is known

Iron Caravan outrider, Routeborn, and the holder of an unbroken delivery record she will mention inside four minutes of meeting you. She rides ahead, she comes back, and the caravan turns on what she says.

What they can do for you

Escort, scouting, and a ten-word message to anywhere the Caravan runs. She will take a party on as crew at rank, which is worth more than it sounds.

Bellisa Vane ♀

Who Waits for Both Moons

Gnome - Vaneborn · Observatory Watcher · Moons Domain Cleric 13

What is known

An Observatory family that turned to the moons two generations back, and Bellisa is what came of it. She keeps the night watch at the Grand Observatory and holds a service afterwards that anyone may attend and almost nobody does.

What they can do for you

Twenty-four hours of exact weather, a Moonbow summoned on a clear night, and healing that does not ask what you were doing when it happened.

Ushra Doorward ♀

Who Holds the Gate and Does Not Watch Herself Do It

Verge Orc · Vault Warden · Path of the Rageform Barbarian 12

What is known

The gate warden at the Verge settlement of Melidion Crossing, and the reason it is still a settlement. Orcs from further out think the Verge names are funny — Doorward, Latchkey, Stayput — right up until they need one of the doors held.

What they can do for you

She will hold a doorway, a bridge, or a stair for as long as it takes, and she will not negotiate the terms afterwards. Feed her and she considers it settled.

Thura Skyward ♀

Who Reads the Sky and Charges for the Reading

Goliath - Vortexborn · Drift Runner · Drifter Ranger 12

What is known

A Drifter out of the vortex peaks who will tell you what the sky is about to do and what it will cost. She takes payment in favours rather than coin, on the grounds that coin is heavy and a favour is not.

What they can do for you

Sky reading — phenomenon, tier, and time remaining — plus escort through anything she has read. She has never lost a client and mentions this constantly.

Ashkora Grey-Under ♀

Who the Shamans Will Not Petition For

Ashenborn - Shroudknit · Wyldermoore Deathwarden · The Scarring Warlock 15

What is known

An Ashenborn who came out of a Necrotic Shroud and did not come out the same. The shamans will not petition on her behalf because they are not certain who would answer. Wyldermoore has a standing warrant and has not served it, because nobody has volunteered to.

What they can do for you

None. She does not sell anything. If she approaches a party it is because she wants something specific from one of them, and she will be pleasant and precise about it.

Corran ♂

Who Answers to Corr, and to Neither

The Echoed - Fractured · Datasphere Diver · Nanite Bloodline Sorcerer 13

What is known

A Fractured Echoed who goes by Corran and by Corr and corrects nobody. He was a respectable Datasphere diver until about two years ago. People who worked with him then describe the change as gradual and then not.

What they can do for you

He recruits. He is generous, genuinely charming, and very good at identifying the one thing a person has not been offered yet.

Marrek Oathwall ♂

Who Kept the Doctrine

Bulwark Orc · Guardian Initiate · Echoing Blade Fighter 15

What is known

A Guardian officer of long service and visible decoration, currently holding a settlement in the Outer Lands that the Guardians say is under his protection. The settlement has not sent anyone out in five months. Officially there is no problem.

What they can do for you

He will hear a delegation. He is courteous, he is not lying, and he will explain his reasoning in full, which is the most disturbing part of meeting him.

Ilvess ♀

Not Detectable, Not Remembered

Human - The Vessin · Memory Broker · Ghost Rogue 11

What is known

There is no public information. Three cities have an open file with no name on it and a consistent method. Two of the files are closed as accidental. The Memory Brokers of Memento will confirm that someone with her description does not work for them, and will not elaborate.

What they can do for you

She takes contracts. There is no way to find her; she finds the client, once, and the conversation happens somewhere the client did not choose.

Oren Lightline ♂

Whose Patron Is a Doorway

The Echoed · Datasphere Diver · The Gate Warlock 12

What is known

An Echoed who opens things and puts them back. If a caravan lost a strongbox in a rift or a family lost an heirloom to a fire, Oren is the name that gets passed along. He is cheerful, competent, and everyone assumes the price is money.

What they can do for you

He restores destroyed objects — actually restores, not repairs — and opens Old World installations. He charges fairly and delivers on time.

Sethra Emberhold ♀

Who Keeps the Damper Closed

Dragonborn - Emberscale · Ashwalker · Warrior of the Ember Monk 10

What is known

A dragonborn of the volcanic country who trained in an Ashenborn discipline that dragonborn are not taught, and nobody will say who taught her. She takes work from war bands and from the cities and has never been caught doing both at once.

What they can do for you

She hires out. She is expensive, she is exact about the terms, and she has never once exceeded a brief — which is the part that should worry you.

Ansem Truecurrent ♂

Around Whom Nothing Works

Aasimar - Sparkborn · Storm Chandler · Storm-Awakened Sorcerer 12

What is known

A storm chandler out of Thundermount who was, by every account, the most gifted device-tender of his generation — until every device he touched began dying instead. He now sells storm prediction and stays away from workshops. People are sorry for him.

What they can do for you

Accurate storm windows, sold cheap, to anyone who asks. He is generous with them and has never taken payment from a crew that could not afford it.

Belden Nevercease ♂

Who Has Not Slept Since the Factory

Dwarf - Aeonbound · Reclaimer Runner · School of the Riftwright Wizard 14

What is known

A Reclaimer wizard who maps the Dimensional Scars and is generous with what he finds. Six survey companies use his charts. He is unfailingly courteous, answers letters the same day, and has been awake for nineteen years.

What they can do for you

He will chart a Scar, date a rift, and tell you which of the two ends moved. His charts are given away free and are better than the ones you would pay for.

Ren Unowed ♀

Whose Name Stopped Being True

Tiefling - Ashwake · Council Page of New Grandia · Lord of the Otherside Warlock 13

What is known

A council page in New Grandia, well liked, good with names, carries messages between the Elders without ever seeming to hear them. She is an Ashwake and she says so within a minute of meeting you, the way all Ashwake do: born there, pacted to nothing.

What they can do for you

She carries council correspondence and knows which Elder is actually deciding a thing. She will tell you, for free, and be right.
